WELCOME TO VELVET CRUMB

Baked with passion, served with warmth.

Velvet Crumb is a cozy bakery and coffee destination created for people who appreciate fresh food, good coffee and beautiful moments.

From buttery croissants and creamy pastries to rich cakes and freshly brewed coffee, every item is prepared with care and attention to detail.

Our goal is simple — to create delicious treats that make ordinary days feel a little more special.
