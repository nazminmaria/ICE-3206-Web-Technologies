BANGLADESH UNIVERSITY OF PROFESSIONALS

Excellence Through Knowledge

Bangladesh University of Professionals (BUP) is a public university located in Mirpur Cantonment, Dhaka.

BUP was established on 5 June 2008 and became the 31st public university of Bangladesh.

The university provides higher education and professional development across a range of academic disciplines.

ACADEMIC ENVIRONMENT

Learning, research and professional development.

BUP offers academic programs across areas including science and technology, business studies, arts and social sciences, security and strategic studies and medical studies.

The university also provides a platform for students to participate in academic activities, competitions, research, clubs and extracurricular programs.
